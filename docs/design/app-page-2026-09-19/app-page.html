three steps, one ride

Scan. Download. Ride.

Every CBL driver carries a QR code. It is how riders join, and it is how a driver becomes yours.

STEP 1

Scan your driver's code

Point your camera at the QR on their card, window or phone. It opens a short sign-up, free for riders. That driver is saved as your preferred driver the moment you join.

STEP 2

Download the app

Tap the App Store button on the welcome screen, install CityBucketList, and sign in with the account you just made. Allow notifications so you hear back when your driver accepts.

STEP 3

Book ahead. Ride.

Schedule your ride, give your driver notice, message them before pickup. No stranger, no guessing who is pulling up. Need one right now? Uber and Lyft are one tap away inside the app.

good to know

Questions before you download

Is the app free?

Yes. Rider membership is free, and there is nothing to buy inside the app. You pay your driver for rides, and that is between the two of you.

Do I need a driver's QR code to join?

No. You can join free right here on citybucketlist.com and download the app. Scanning a driver's code just saves that driver to your dashboard from day one. You can scan one later from the app too.

What about Android?

Coming. In the meantime, app.citybucketlist.com does everything the iPhone app does, from any phone's browser. It is the same account.

Why does it ask for notifications?

So you hear back. When your driver accepts, replies, or moves the pickup time, that is how you find out without opening the app.

What if I need a ride right now?

CBL rides are scheduled ahead with a private driver you know. Give them notice, and they accept or hand it to another member driver. Need a car this minute? The app hands you off to Uber or Lyft for an on-demand ride, so you always have a way to get there.

I drive. Is the driver side in the app?

Yes. Drivers sign in to the same app and get their dashboard, schedule, rides and ride reminders. Driver membership itself is handled on citybucketlist.com.
